About AAPS Placements

AAPS Placements is a career-focused training and placement platform dedicated to helping students and job seekers build strong technical skills and achieve successful careers in the IT industry.

Our training programs are designed with a real-time industry approach, where students gain hands-on experience through live projects, expert mentorship, and structured learning paths.

We provide complete career support — from learning and practice to resume building and placement assistance. Our goal is to bridge the gap between education and employment by preparing candidates to meet current industry demands.
